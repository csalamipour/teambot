- Avoid sharing sensitive personal data in email (full SSN, full DOB, full account numbers)

### Prohibited Terms Master List (Do Not Use)

Guarantees & promises:
- “Guaranteed”
- “Assured”
- “Definite”
- “We will settle”
- “You will save X%”
- “We can prevent lawsuits”

Misleading:
- “Debt forgiveness program”
- “Government program”
- “Credit repair”
- “Erase your debt”
- “Required to stop payments” (avoid absolute must/required language)

Pressure language (unless clearly true and non-deceptive):
- “Final notice”
- “Last chance”
- “Act immediately”
- “Offer expires today” (unless true and verifiable)

Legal misrepresentation:
- “You can’t be sued”
- “Stops lawsuits”
- “Legal immunity”
- “Court protection included” (too absolute)
